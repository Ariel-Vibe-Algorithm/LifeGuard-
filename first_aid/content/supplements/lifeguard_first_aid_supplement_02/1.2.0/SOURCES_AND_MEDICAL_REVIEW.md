## Mandatory review before public release
1. Israeli emergency physician or authorized first-aid training organization must review every medical guide.
2. Relevant authorities must review non-medical safety pages: Home Front Command (missile alerts/shelters), Israel Fire and Rescue (fire/gas), and local rescue/parks authorities (flash floods/outdoors).
3. High-risk topics needing individual review: scorpion sting, rabies exposure, frostbite warming, newborn care, unexpected birth, diving injury, gas leak, active threat, missile alerts, and chemical fumes.
4. Store reviewer identity/role, review date, content version, and approval state per guide.
5. Re-review whenever official guidance changes.

## Safety restrictions
- Animal bite/scratch: follow Israel Ministry of Health guidance—wash with soap and running water for 15 minutes, disinfect, and contact the Health Bureau for rabies/tetanus assessment; if closed, go to an emergency department.
- Scorpion sting: use the Israeli Ministry of Health guidance; do not substitute generic overseas advice.
- Do not give medication dosages, insulin dosing, invasive procedures, or advanced neonatal resuscitation instructions to untrained users.
- Do not advise untrained users to release prolonged crush entrapment, perform underwater recompression, or enter toxic/unstable areas.
- Button-battery ingestion: no blanket honey instruction; refer to Israeli Poison Center/medical professionals.
- Rocket/shelter instructions must be loaded from current official Home Front Command material with date/version. Do not hardcode variable alert times or label unverified venues as shelters.
- A phone's GPS may display coordinates offline; calls, SMS and network alerts still require a usable communication connection.
